FINANCE FAQ

Questions?

Can I finance a used car?

Yes. Financing may be available for qualifying pre-owned vehicles depending on vehicle age, condition and lender requirements.

How much should I put down?

The ideal down payment depends on your budget and financing situation. A larger down payment can reduce the amount you need to finance.

How long does approval take?

Approval times vary depending on the lender, application and documentation. Our finance team will guide you through the process.

Can I trade in my current vehicle?

Yes. You can trade in your current vehicle and use its estimated value toward the purchase of another vehicle.

Can I apply before choosing a car?

Depending on the finance program, you may be able to discuss your financing options before selecting your vehicle.
